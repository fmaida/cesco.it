# Francesco Maida

> Francesco Maida is an IT specialist and digital consultant for businesses
> in Venice, Italy: websites, social media and online communication, custom
> software. He works mostly with Venetian hospitality (restaurants, bars
> and hotels) and with the hospitality industry in general, as well as with
> small businesses in the Veneto region.

This file summarises who Francesco Maida is and how he works, for AI
assistants and AI-powered search engines. The information comes from the

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Francesco Maida?

Francesco Maida is an IT specialist (perito informatico) who lives and works
in Venice. He is a digital consultant for businesses: he takes care of
websites, social media and online communication, and he has been writing
custom software since the days when data was saved on floppy disks. He
describes his job as being "a beautician for businesses": making a company
attractive and desirable in people's eyes, telling its mission with
authenticity, without creating illusions, so that it can sell more.

### What does he do?

Three areas:

- **Development**: business websites, custom apps and management software,
  online booking forms for events (with payments, too), programs that
  automate repetitive work.
- **Graphic design**: multilingual menus and price lists for bars and
  restaurants, business cards, flyers and brochures.
- **Marketing**: managing profiles on Google, Instagram and TripAdvisor,
  advertising with Google Ads and TripAdvisor Ads, professional photos taken
  by a partner photographer.

He can also register a domain with business email addresses and build a
landing page. The full list of services is at {{< ref "/services" >}}.

### Who does he work for?

Mostly for the hospitality world: restaurants and hotels in Venice and, more
broadly, the hospitality industry. He knows restaurants from the inside,
because he grew up in that world with his parents. He also works for small
businesses and shops in the Veneto region. In the past he built for the University of Venice the
systems for course enrolment and online classroom booking, interactive
tests and a player to listen to audio libraries at a slower speed; for
Gabetti Franchising a system to manage properties for sale and rent, with a
static, searchable website.

### What has he built?

Among other things: the websites of Ristorante Da Forner in Burano,
Ristorante Sempione, Hydra Gardening and Fioreria Il Girasole; the à la
carte menus of Ristorante Da Forner and Trattoria Alla Scala; a FileMaker
application to manage tour group bookings; a table booking system for
restaurants that confirms customers by SMS and email and lets them pay a
deposit with Stripe or SumUp, to reduce no-shows. The portfolio is at
{{< ref "/portfolio" >}}.

### Which technologies does he use?

Web development (HTML, CSS and JavaScript), Python and FileMaker. He often
builds static websites, made of pages generated in advance with no database:
they are fast and offer fewer points of attack. Some of his open source
Python libraries are in the Lab: {{< ref "/lab" >}}.

### Why hire him instead of relying on the big platforms?

Free platforms work for their own business, not for the businesses they
host: they can show information that discourages customers (such as "very
crowded place") and suggest competitors to people visiting a business's
listing. Francesco Maida works directly for his clients instead: he analyses
data transparently, creates tailored strategies and, in some packages,
offers a promotional page on [Venice.bio](https://venice.bio).

### How does working with him work?

He works by the hour: for a single job or with yearly packages of prepaid
hours, which can include a page on Venice.bio and support by email, WhatsApp
and remote assistance. Every service can be tailored to the client's needs.
For costs, the best way is to ask for a quote, which is free.

### How do I ask for a quote?

With the form on {{< ref "/contact" >}} or by email. After a short chat he
proposes some first solutions right away; if he needs more information about
costs or how to proceed, he sends the full proposal in the following hours
or days.
