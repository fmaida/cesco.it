My name is Francesco Maida, I live in Venice and I'm an IT specialist.

Think of me as a beautician for businesses: I work with care and dedication to make your company attractive and desirable in people's eyes, so that you can sell more. I don't dress things up or create illusions: I bring out the best of your business, telling its mission with authenticity and passion.

I've been writing custom software since the days when data was saved on floppy disks. For the University of Venice I built systems for course enrolment, online classroom booking and tools for students: interactive self-assessment tests and a player for listening to audio libraries at reduced speed.

For Gabetti Franchising I created a system to manage properties for sale and for rent, with a static, searchable website: no database, no doors left open for those who want to do damage, just pages generated in advance and a small internal search engine.

Today I work mostly with Venetian restaurants, a world I know from the inside because it's the one I grew up in, my parents' world. I developed a table booking system that confirms customers by text message and email and lets them pay a deposit with Stripe or SumUp, to reduce no-shows.
